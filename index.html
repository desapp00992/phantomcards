How It Works

Your journey from purchase to withdrawal in 6 simple steps.

1

Browse the marketplace

Explore sealed cards priced in USD from $3 to $50. Choose by category, style, and price.

2

Deposit, then buy

Use Paystack for wallet deposits only. Card purchases deduct from your verified wallet balance.

3

Receive your code

After payment, you'll get a unique alphanumeric redemption code.

4

Redeem the code

Enter your code in the Redeem section. Your wallet will be credited in GHS.

5

Reveal your card credit

Redeem your code after purchase to reveal the wallet credit assigned to that sealed card.

6

Withdraw to mobile money

Add a withdrawal method, set your PIN, and request a withdrawal to MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, or AT Cash.

Card values stay sealed until redemption. Pick the card you want, buy from wallet balance, then redeem the code to credit redeemed balance.
